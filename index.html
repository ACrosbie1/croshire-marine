What We Do

Yacht Consultantage Services

Whether you are selling your vessel or searching for your next one, we manage the entire transaction on your behalf.

Sellers
Sell Your Vessel
We list your vessel across all major yacht marketplaces, handle inquiries, qualify serious buyers, coordinate the marine survey and sea trial, and negotiate on your behalf. You receive offers. We do the work.
Buyers
Find Your Vessel
Tell us what you need. We search active and off-market listings, review vessel history and condition reports, arrange surveys, and protect your interests through every stage of the purchase.
Valuation
Know What It's Worth
Using BUCValu and live market comparables, we deliver an accurate valuation before any offer is made. You negotiate with confidence, not guesswork.
Inspection
Marine Survey & Sea Trial
We coordinate a certified NAMS or SAMS marine surveyor and sea trial on every vessel before closing. Every finding is disclosed and negotiated into the final price. No surprises after the deal is done.
Title
USCG Title & Escrow
We run a clean USCG documentation search, verify no liens exist, and coordinate escrow through licensed marine title specialists. Every closing is protected and documented.
Fleet
Fleet & International
Acquiring a charter fleet or sourcing from Caribbean and European markets? We manage the full pipeline across domestic and international inventory for operators and corporate buyers.

About Us

Built on Relationships. Driven by Results.

Croshire Marine was founded on one principle -- that buying or selling a vessel should be simple, transparent, and stress-free.

We are a commission-only consultantage. That means we have no upfront fees, no listing charges, and no retainers. Our success is tied directly to yours. We earn only when your deal closes.

From single engine pistons to corporate jets, we manage every step of the transaction -- valuation, marketing, buyer qualification, pre-purchase inspection, title search, and escrow coordination. You focus on the water. We handle everything else.
